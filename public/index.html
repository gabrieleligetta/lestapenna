How it works

From voice chat
to campaign lore.

A deliberate four-step flow keeps the table in control—from starting the recording to reviewing the finished chronicle.

  1. Connect the table

    During the private beta, we help configure the Discord server and campaign.

  2. Start deliberately

    The DM starts recording only after the group is ready and informed.

  3. Play as usual

    Lestapenna captures each participant and processes the session when it ends.

  4. Return to the story

    Read the recap and explore the updated campaign archive from Discord or the web.

Recording with intent

The table always knows when the bard is listening.

Recording is a deliberate session action, never a background feature. Server administrators remain responsible for informing participants and obtaining the consent required for their table and jurisdiction.

Read our recording and data policy →

Recording starts only on command
  • ✓No passive listening. Lestapenna does not record outside an explicitly active session.
  • ✓Visible state. The bot joins the voice channel while the session is active.
  • ✓A clear choice. Anyone who does not consent should not join, or should leave, the recorded channel.
  • ✓Purpose-bound processing. Audio exists to create the transcript, recap and campaign memory.

Free, and open source

We earn nothing.
You bring the AI keys.

Lestapenna is a personal effort donated to the tabletop community. There is no subscription, no credits, no payment of any kind: the software is free and its source is public under AGPL-3.0. The AI runs on your own provider keys, so what it costs is charged straight to your account — and you can see the estimate before every action.

Shared public bot: the free hardware currently supports at most two simultaneous recordings across two Discord servers. For dedicated capacity and smoother continuity, self-host your own instance; donations help us invest in shared cloud infrastructure.

What we do not do: we receive no money, we sell no data, and there is no analytics or tracking script anywhere in this project. Third-party services are named explicitly in the privacy policy: Discord, Oracle Cloud (Milan) for storage, and the AI provider you choose — where the relationship is directly between you and them.

Questions from the tavern

Before you invite the bard.

We are opening access gradually while the authenticated web dashboard and Discord publication flow are completed.

What does it actually cost me?

Nothing to us. The AI runs on your provider key, so you pay OpenAI or Google directly — a four-hour session is typically a couple of euros, and the estimate is shown before you start. If you run transcription and models on your own machine, it costs nothing at all.

Who profits from this?

Nobody. There is no company, no revenue and no investor behind Lestapenna: it is one person's project, released under AGPL-3.0 so it stays open for whoever comes next. Sponsorship on GitHub is welcome but buys no feature.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. The source is public and the whole stack runs with Docker. On your own server nothing leaves your machine except the calls to the AI provider you configure.

What data do you collect?

What a session needs: the audio you explicitly record, its transcript, and the campaign material generated from it. No analytics, no tracking, no profiling — and nothing is ever sold or shared. The privacy policy lists every third party involved.
