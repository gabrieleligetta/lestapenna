Cookies

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Short version: one cookie, and only after you log in. No banner is shown because there is nothing to consent to — a strictly necessary session cookie does not require consent under the ePrivacy Directive, and we set nothing else.

What we set

NamePurposeLife
lp_session Keeps you logged in to the web app after you sign in with Discord. Without it you would be asked to sign in on every page. 30 days, or until you log out

It is HttpOnly (JavaScript cannot read it), Secure and SameSite-restricted. It contains an opaque identifier, not your data.

What we do not set

No analytics cookie, no advertising cookie, no third-party pixel, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracker of any kind. The public pages you are reading right now set nothing at all — you can check with your browser's developer tools, and you can check the source, which is public.

Elsewhere

Logging in goes through Discord, which sets its own cookies on its own domain under its own policy. We have no control over those and receive nothing from them.
